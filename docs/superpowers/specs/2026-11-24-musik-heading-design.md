# Musik heading: big "Musik" instead of "Dengarkan Sekarang"

Date: 2026-11-24 · Status: approved · Size: small change

- **Why:** a shorter, bolder heading so the focus goes to the Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube buttons.
- **Goal:** the Musik section heading reads "Musik" (EN "Music"), same size and white style as the other section headings, on one line.
- **Non-goals:** no change to the buttons, the Spotify player or the layout.
- **Anti-goal:** the word appearing twice; the small gold "Musik" label above the heading is removed.
- **Approaches:** (1) big "Musik", no label (chosen: least text competing with the buttons); (2) big "Musik" with "Dengarkan Sekarang" as a small label above (keeps the invitation but adds a line of text).
- **Left to the builder:** drop the now-unused `music.label` field.
